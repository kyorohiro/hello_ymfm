# about ym2612

```
http://www.chipdir.nl/pinusr/ym2612.txt


    +--()--+
GND | 1  24| 0M(CLK)
 D0 | 2  23| Vcc
 D1 | 3  22| A.Vcc
 D2 | 4  21| MOL
 D3 | 5  20| MOR
 D4 | 6  19| A.GND
 D5 | 7  18| A1
 D6 | 8  17| A0
 D7 | 9  16| /RD
  ? |10  15| /WR
/IC |11  14| /CS
GND |12  13| /IRQ
    +------+
```

```
GND: ground 
A.GND: ground 
Vcc: power supply
A.Vcc: power supply
CLK: clock input

MOL: left audio output pins.
MOR: right audio output pins.
```

```
Q: Why are there two types of ground, GND and A.GND?
A: The analog section is used for audio output. It handles the part of the chip that turns the internal sound data into an audio signal.
A.VCC and A.GND are used for the analog audio output section, including the circuitry behind MOL and MOR.

```
